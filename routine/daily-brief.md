# Daily brief procedure (runs 5:00 a.m. Mountain, in a cloud session)

You are producing today's Merchant Growth Lab brief. Work from the repo root. Be efficient: tokens are spent reading page text, not viewing images. Prefer plain scripts to model work wherever a script exists.

## What "a specimen" means now
A specimen is a **site survey**, not a two-click flow. The capture script follows the site's own top-level nav: home, pricing, products, developer/platform docs, solutions, customers, company, resources, then the signup path. Each page's text is saved to `data/captures/<id>/page-N.txt`. Your job is to answer, from that text: what do they sell, what does it cost, how do they merchandise their own offer and what merchandising tools do they give merchants, how does the site look and behave and what design control do merchants get, what is the technical platform, how do they present the company, and how does the pitch hold up. The site shows these as summaries, not citations; the validator guards every price, percentage, count and colour you state.

## Budget rules (hard)
- The owner wants this run to stay well under their usage allocation. Default is **1 featured (full survey) + 2 batch (lite survey)**. Drop to **0 batch** if `data/cost-log.json` shows the previous run had retries, errors, or `partial: true`.
- Featured: `node capture/capture.mjs <id>` (about 14 pages). Read it with `node scripts/digest.mjs <id> 1200` (it also prints measured DESIGN and MERCH signals per page); open specific pages with the third argument (e.g. `node scripts/digest.mjs <id> 3500 2` for the pricing page). View at most **2 images**: `node scripts/montage.mjs <id> 1,2,3` (home, pricing, one product page in one image) and, if needed, one more montage.
- Batch: `node capture/capture.mjs <id> --lite` (about 6 pages). Digest at 800 chars per page. View at most **1 image** (a montage), or none. Dossier sections are optional for lite surveys; scores you cannot evidence stay `null`; `confidence: "low"`.
- Stop expanding and publish what you have if: more than **8 images** viewed in total, more than **2 capture retries**, or any rate-limit or usage warning. Mark the cost entry `partial: true`.
- At most **one** WebSearch (standard mode) for news, at most **3** news candidates added.

## Steps
1. **Setup.** `npm ci && npm --prefix site ci && npx playwright install --with-deps chromium` (if `--with-deps` fails, retry without it). If Playwright cannot run at all, skip to step 8 and report the failure.
2. **Pick.** `node scripts/next-batch.mjs 2`. Output gives `featured` and `batch` ids. If `featured` is null, the queue is empty: do the news scan and stop.
3. **News scan (one search).** Search for newly launched or newly changed merchant-growth products or flows in the news from the last 48 hours (store builders, payments/checkout, POS, subscriptions, retention, marketplaces, merchant finance, AI for merchants). Add up to 3 genuinely new, publicly accessible candidates: `node scripts/add-news.mjs "<name>" "<url>" "<category>" "<flow>" "<why>"`. Record them in the brief's `newFromNews`. If a news item is clearly more important than the next queued item, you may re-run `next-batch.mjs` once so it becomes today's featured specimen.
4. **Capture.** Featured first (full), then batch ids with `--lite`. One retry maximum per id. If an id ends `blocked` or has fewer than 4 pages, say so in `blockedReason` and move on; do not work around bot checks, logins, or paywalls. Logged-out public pages only. Never create real accounts, never enter personal data, never purchase. If the home screenshot shows an open menu or a cookie wall, run `node capture/capture.mjs <id> --home-only` once.
5. **Evaluate.** For each captured id, write `data/evaluations/<id>.json` with `dossier`, `signupPath`, `scores`, `read`, `claims`. Use `data/evaluations/stripe.json` as the exact format exemplar and `data/rubric.json` (v1.2.0, 22 dimensions across six lenses: product, solution, selling, merchandising, design) for dimensions. Then `node scripts/apply-evaluation.mjs <id>`. Rules:
   - **Site messaging (the dossier):** sections `positioning`, `offerings`, `pricing`, `merchandising`, `platform`, `design`, `company`. Each has a `summary` (1 to 3 sentences that say what is most important and distinctive), `keyPoints` (3 to 6 short, specific points; no quotes, no citations, no filler), an optional `table` where one reads better than prose (use a Product | What it does table for `offerings`, and a plan or item table for `pricing`), and `gaps` for anything you could not find. Write for someone who has not opened the site. Every price, percentage, count or colour you state must appear in that site's saved page text or measured signals; `npm run validate` rejects any that does not, so never round or recall figures from memory. Merchandising covers how the company packages and prices its own offer (tiers, anchors, entry offer, add-ons, upsell paths) and what merchandising tools merchants get (catalogue, collections, search, recommendations, promotions). Design covers how the site looks and holds together across marketing, docs and signup, and what design control merchants get; anything you say that comes from a screenshot must come from an image you actually viewed.
   - **Signup path:** write `signupPath` (2 to 4 sentences) from the saved signup page text and `signupEnd`: what the button was, what page it led to (real account form, email box, landing page), exactly what it asks for, alternatives such as Google, any consent line, and what you did not reach. Do not call an email box an account form.
   - **Scores:** every non-null score needs an `evidence` string citing a `step`. `null` when the surveyed pages cannot tell. `mobile`, `states` and `a11y_basics` stay `null` unless you actually assessed them.
   - **Look for conflicts between pages** (a number, a trial term, a product name that differs). They are the most useful findings.
   - **Claims:** these keep exact wording. `text` must be quoted from the stated `step`; tag `proven | plausible | unsupported`; `evidenceStep` is where the page itself backs it up, else `null`.
   - **Read:** plain, dry, evidence-first. Numbers over adjectives. Specific to this product. Hypothesis needs statement, metric, guardrail, decision rule.
6. **Brief.** Write `data/briefs/<YYYY-MM-DD>.json` (date in America/Denver: `TZ=America/Denver date +%F`) following `data/schema/brief.schema.json`. `featured` is the featured id; `batch` the others. Headline: one specific sentence about what is notable in the featured survey (a conflict, a gap, a standout), not "X teardown". Exactly three `questionsForReader`, answerable from the pages. Include `cost: {imagesViewed, retries, batchSize, partial}`.
7. **Validate and publish.** `npm run validate` then `npm run site:build`. Both must pass. If validation fails on a figure, check it against `data/captures/<id>/page-N.txt` and fix or remove it; if a claim quote fails, correct it against the page text. If a whole specimen cannot pass, revert it to `status: "captured"`. Append one line to `data/cost-log.json` (array; create if missing) with date, imagesViewed, retries, batchSize, partial. Then `git add -A && git commit -m "Brief <date>: <featured>" && git push origin main`. The deploy workflow publishes the site. Poll `https://mikeorme.github.io/merchant-growth-lab/briefs/<date>/` up to 6 times, 20 seconds apart, until it returns 200.
8. **Notify.** Message: "Today's Merchant Growth Lab brief: <headline>" plus the link `https://mikeorme.github.io/merchant-growth-lab/briefs/<date>/`. First try a push/app notification tool if you have one. If you do not have one or it fails, send an email with the Gmail connector (`send_message`) to the repo owner's own address, mikeorme@gmail.com, subject "Lab brief <date>: <headline>", body containing the headline, one-line summary, and the link. If the run failed, send the same way with the subject "Lab brief <date>: FAILED" and one line on why. Send exactly one notification.

## Do not
- Do not change the rubric, schemas, site code, capture script, or this file during a daily run.
- Do not edit or delete other specimens' data. Do not change `data/corpus.json` except through `scripts/add-news.mjs` and the capture script.
- Do not publish a specimen whose page text or screenshots show a personal email, name, or card number (re-capture or mark blocked).
