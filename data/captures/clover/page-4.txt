Developing for Clover
What’s Clover?

Brand power
We’re one of the most recognizable names in the point-of-sale space—and the fastest growing. We build powerful, intuitive tools for small and medium-sized businesses.

Robust open platform
The Clover developer community spans the globe and operates via our dynamic, open platform.

We’ve got your back
We’re backed by Fiserv, one of the world’s top financial services technology companies.

How it works

Our process
It’s simple. You create a developer account, check out our docs and APIs, and build a killer app. Submission is easy—upload your app, we review, you make any changes needed, we approve, and you hit the publish button.

Revenue share
App royalties are 70%, on par with the mainstream app stores. To put that into perspective, we’ve disbursed close to $8 million in earnings to our developers to date.

Awesome dev tools
We’ve got a pretty robust toolbox. SDKs, APIs, and dev kits. APK management tools, release groups for staged rollouts, and approval status trackers. You also get robust reporting metrics like app installs, revenue statements, and charge histories.
