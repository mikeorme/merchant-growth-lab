Our Story

Founded in 2011 after co-founder Tomer Tagrin was misled by fake reviews, Yotpo helps eCommerce brands build trust and loyalty through authentic customer voices.

What began as a reviews platform now powers two critical parts of the customer journey: Reviews that build credibility and Loyalty programs that drive repeat engagement.As commerce evolves toward AI-powered, personalized experiences, we help brands stand out.​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​
