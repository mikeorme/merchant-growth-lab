FAQs
How much does Yotpo cost?

Yotpo pricing depends on which products you use and your monthly order volume. Reviews & UGC has a limited free plan (up to 50 monthly orders) and paid plans starting at $15/month; Loyalty & Referrals has a free plan with paid tiers starting at $199/month; Discover is custom-priced around your AI visibility goals. You can also bundle reviews and loyalty to save. Use the order-volume slider on each product page to see your exact price.

What products does Yotpo offer, and can I buy just one?

Yotpo has three core products — Reviews & UGC, Loyalty & Referrals, and Discover (AI visibility) – plus a bundle that combines reviews and loyalty. You can start with a single product and add others as you grow, or bundle reviews and loyalty together for a lower combined price.

How is Yotpo pricing calculated?

Reviews and loyalty pricing scales with your store’s monthly order volume, so you only pay for the scale you’re at. Each product page has a slider – set your monthly orders and the matching plan and price appear. Discover and enterprise plans are custom-quoted.

Is there a free plan or a free trial?

Yes. Yotpo Loyalty & Referrals has a full free plan you can start on right away, and Reviews & UGC offers a limited free plan for up to 50 monthly orders. For Discover and the bundle, the best next step is a quick demo so we can match you to the right tier.

Do I need to talk to sales to get pricing?

No – starter and Pro pricing is shown transparently on each product page. You only need to contact us for Premium, enterprise, Discover, and custom bundles, where pricing is tailored to your catalog, markets, and goals.

Can I bundle products to save money?

Yes. Combining Reviews & UGC and Loyalty & Referrals in the bundle costs less than buying each product separately. See the bundle page for combined pricing.
