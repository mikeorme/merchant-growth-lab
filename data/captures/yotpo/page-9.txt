FAQs
What makes a review valuable for AI?

LLMs look for detailed, authentic, structured content, not just star ratings. Reviews with real product experience, photos, attributes, and specific customer language give AI stronger signals to trust and cite.

Why do authentic reviews matter in the AI era?

AI can recommend products, but it relies on real human signals to understand what shoppers actually experience. Authentic reviews provide the fit, feel, taste, use case, and context AI cannot know on its own.

How does Yotpo make reviews easier for LLMs to use?

Yotpo structures and surfaces review content through AI-powered summaries, smart sorting, review highlights, topic clustering, and crawlable content that AI platforms can interpret and cite.

What’s the connection between reviews and Discover?
