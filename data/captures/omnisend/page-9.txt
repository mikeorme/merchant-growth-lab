FAQ
What is an agency partnership?

Agency partnerships are tailored for marketing agencies and freelance specialists looking to expand their offerings with email and SMS marketing automation through Omnisend.

How much can I earn as a partner?

You can earn at least 20% of the monthly Omnisend subscription fees from the clients you refer. Your earnings grow as you bring in more clients and help them succeed.

Is the revenue share a one-time payment or recurring?

It’s recurring! You’ll continue to earn a share of your referred clients’ monthly fees for as long as they use Omnisend.

When can I cash out my earnings?

You can cash out your earnings at any time. Simply log in to the Partner Portal and follow the instructions to withdraw your funds.

How does the agency directory work?

Approved agencies are featured in our directory, connecting them with potential clients searching for expert ecommerce marketing services.

Can my team access the Partner Portal?

Yes, you can invite team members to the Partner Portal, giving them access to manage client accounts and track progress.

What benefits do agencies receive?

Agencies gain revenue sharing, referrals, co-marketing opportunities, early access to new features, and expert training resources.
