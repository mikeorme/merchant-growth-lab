FAQ
What is an email marketing report?

Email marketing reporting shows how your email campaigns perform. It includes key metrics like opens, clicks, sales, unsubscribes, and conversions — helping you understand what’s working and what needs improvement. With Omnisend, these reports are generated automatically for every campaign and automation you send.

How to create an email marketing report with Omnisend?

You don’t need to build reports manually — Omnisend creates them for you. Just send a campaign or set up an automation, and you'll get real-time performance data, visual click maps, and revenue attribution. You can also compare results, filter by date or segment, and export reports anytime.

What metrics should be included in an email marketing analysis report?

A strong email marketing software with reporting/analytics feature should include open rates, click rates, conversion rates, revenue generated, unsubscribe rates, and bounce data. Omnisend also adds ecommerce-focused metrics like sales per email, product performance, and automation flow stats — so you can connect your emails directly to results.

How does Omnisend help identify effective marketing strategies?

Omnisend provides detailed automation and email campaign reporting, helping you see which tactics drive sales and engagement.

How can I track the performance of individual messages within automations?

Omnisend’s message reports display key performance stats in the Automation Editor, enabling data-driven workflow optimization.

Does Omnisend offer insights into the engagement of different customer segments?

Yes, Omnisend's email marketing reporting software highlights which segments engage most, helping you focus your efforts where they matter.

What tools does Omnisend provide for monitoring audience growth?

Omnisend lets you monitor and compare email and SMS list growth from your dashboard, with detailed form reports to assess the effectiveness of your signup forms.
