FAQ
It’s my first time trying email marketing. Can I use Omnisend for free?

Yes, you can sign up for our Free plan and use all Omnisend features for an unlimited time and send up to 500 emails/month. Send your email campaigns, collect new subscribers with our forms, set up automations to do the hard work for you, and segment your audience to personalize the reach. You can upgrade to a paid plan whenever you feel ready.

Will the Free plan give me access to all the features of your paid plans?

Not all of them. The Free plan covers the core: pre-built ecommerce workflows, segmentation, A/B testing, pop-ups, landing pages, and 24/7 support. You can send 500 emails and 500 web push notifications a month to up to 250 contacts. Paid plans add more sending volume and features like custom workflows and Forms AI. SMS and MMS are on the Pro plan. Rates start at $0.007 per SMS and vary by country and volume.

I can’t decide: should I choose the Pro plan or Standard?

Top reasons why customers choose the Pro plan over Standard include unlimited emails, SMS at volume-based rates starting at $0.007, priority support from a dedicated account expert, and advanced reporting. Still have questions or not sure which plan is best for you? Request a 15 minute meeting with our team here → omnisend.com/demo/.

How can I pay?

We accept VISA (credit, debit, prepaid), MasterCard (credit, debit, prepaid), and American Express (credit, prepaid) card payments. Discover, Diners Club, JCB, UnionPay cards are not supported.

This will be my first time setting up an automation workflow. Can I expect any help?

Absolutely. Our award-winning 24/7 support team is always available to assist you and answer any questions.

What if I need to send more emails than my monthly limit?

If you exceed your email sending limit on the Free or Standard plan, we’ll give you the option to upgrade your plan (and we’ll send you a friendly email too). You’ll always be asked to confirm an account upgrade before it happens. Customers on the Pro plan can send unlimited emails per month, however, our fair use policy applies in order to comply with our anti-spam policy.

Can I send MMS?

Yes, MMS is available so you can insert images or gifs into your text messages. MMS is available for US & Canada recipients only, while SMS (text-only messages) is available globally.

Do you offer migration support?

We move everything. Your lists, segments, flows, templates — all of it. You give us read access to your account. We do the rest in just a few days. No charge, no hassle, no learning curve.

I’d like to get dedicated support to set everything up. Can I get an Account Expert?

All Standard and Pro plan customers with spending $400/month or more get a dedicated Account Expert who provides migration support, helps set your first automations and campaigns, and provides ongoing personalized support to make sure you’re making the most of Omnisend.

I have more questions. How can I reach you?

Simply send us an email at support@omnisend.com. We check it 24/7 and we’ll be happy to answer any of your questions.
