FAQ
What is Social Capture?

Social Capture is an Omnisend feature that turns Instagram comments and DMs into email subscribers. When someone comments or DMs a word you’ve chosen, it messages them, asks for their email, and adds them to your Omnisend audience.

How is it different from Omnisend sign-up forms?

Signup forms collect subscribers from people who visit your website. Social Capture reaches people on Instagram, including those who haven’t visited your store yet. The two work well together: one covers your site, the other your social audience.

What do I need to get started?

You need a paid Omnisend plan and an Instagram business account, either business or creator. Connect your Instagram account from the Social Capture page in Omnisend, and you’re ready to set up your first trigger.

What triggers the DM?

A word you choose, like “link” or “drop.” When someone comments it on your post or sends it to your brand in a DM, Social Capture replies automatically. You can use comments, DMs, or both as triggers.

Do people know they’re subscribing?

Yes. Social Capture asks for their email directly in the DM, and the message includes a disclaimer explaining they’re joining your list. Only people who reply with their email are added as subscribers.

Can I write my own DM?

Yes. Every message is fully customizable, so you can write it in your brand’s voice. You can also include a discount code as a thank-you for subscribing, or leave it out.

Do I need to run Instagram ads?

No. Social Capture works on your organic posts and DMs, so the engagement you already get becomes a way to grow your email list, without spending anything on ads.

Can I see how many subscribers came from Instagram?

Yes, during Social Capture setup you can add a tag of your choice, like “Instagram.” This makes it easy to spot those who came from Instagram in their contact profile. If you choose not to add a tag, you can still find them through the Subscribed to marketing or Opted-in events.

Which Omnisend plans include Social Capture?

Social Capture is available on all paid Omnisend plans. If you’re on the free plan, you can upgrade anytime from your account to start collecting email subscribers from Instagram. Check our pricing page for plan details.
