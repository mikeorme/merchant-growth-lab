What is the Amazon Seller mobile app?
The Amazon Seller mobile app is a free mobile application that allows Amazon sellers to manage their business and grow sales from anywhere. The app syncs with your Amazon selling account so you can track orders, add or update listings, manage inventory, add user permissions, and more—all from the convenience of your mobile device.

Use the app to help you save time, keep your business running smoothly, and scale.
Go mobile
Ready to manage your business on the go?
Download the Amazon Seller mobile app for iPhone on the Apple Store or for Android on Google Play.
5 key features in the Amazon Seller mobile app
1. Search and add products with barcode scanning
Simply scan a barcode or point your device’s camera at a product to see if it’s already available in the Amazon store—then list it. If it’s not sold in the Amazon store, you can start the process of adding a new product. Learn more about how to use barcode scanning for product research, reselling, and more.
2. Global selling
The app gives you global coverage with a single sign-on and the ability to switch seamlessly across the countries and regions where you sell. Learn more about global selling with Amazon.
3. Customizable notifications
Turn on notifications so you don’t miss important updates or time-sensitive communications. Make selections based on what type of notifications you want to receive on your mobile device, and adjust settings to suit your needs.
4. Seller App Photo Studio
Take, edit, and save professional-quality product photos using the Amazon Product Photo Studio. Make sure photos meet Amazon’s standards for product listing content, and fix issues in the app.
5. Seller Support
Amazon Seller Support is here to answer your questions and provide the info you need to get started and sell successfully in the Amazon store. You can contact Seller Support directly through the app.

8 ways to use the Amazon Seller mobile app
1. Stay current on updates
On the app homepage you can find policy updates and other types of news and announcements. Watch for banner alerts related to your account health, and learn about actions you can take to improve your business performance and meet targets.
2. Monitor sales
Analyze sales growth at a glance, then drill down to product-level sales data over time. Watch key performance metrics, review business reports, view payments, and find current and past statements all in one place.
3. Find products to sell
Whenever inspiration strikes, research products by looking up ASINs, scanning barcodes, or pointing your camera at the product to find it. You can compare competitor offers and also dig into product categories for “Top Sellers” or “Hot New Releases.”
Learn more about how to get product ideas
4. Create and edit product listings
Add new products to your inventory and make adjustments to your current offers right in the app. You can find an existing listing and create a new offer for a product already in the Amazon store, or make a new listing if the product isn’t currently in the Amazon catalog. You can also adjust prices and make other tweaks to product content.
5. Manage inventory and returns
See inventory status throughout the day and adjust quantity levels when stock is getting low. You can also view returns, authorize return requests, and contact buyers directly from the app.
Learn more about inventory management
6. Handle fulfillment and shipping
Based on your fulfillment channels, you can monitor orders, confirm shipment for orders you fulfill on your own, and track shipments through Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA), a program that allows you to ship products into Amazon’s fulfillment network and outsource the packing, shipping, customer service, and returns.
7. Manage customer communications
View customer feedback and post public replies to customer reviews. Respond to buyer messages, get notifications for messages that might need your attention, and track customer support cases.
8. Run advertising campaigns
Track ad spend, impressions, clicks, and more metrics to evaluate performance for Sponsored Products and other types of ads. You can also update budgets, modify keywords, and pause individual campaigns.
Learn more about how the Amazon Seller mobile app works
Learn the lingo:
Featured Offer
In the Amazon store, multiple sellers can offer the same product through a single product detail page. The Amazon Featured Offer is the offer that appears at the top right of a product detail page with Buy Now and Add to Cart buttons.

You can use the Amazon Seller mobile app to see the current Featured Offer for products you may want to list and adjust pricing to remain competitive.
Learn more about the Featured Offer
How to get the Amazon Seller mobile app
1. Create an account
Before you can get started with the app, you’ll need to create an Amazon selling account. Start by choosing a selling plan, then register as a seller.
2. Download the app
Visit the app store of your choice, or log in to Seller Central, scroll to the footer of the homepage, and select Download the Amazon Seller mobile app.
3. Start exploring
Open the app to sync with your selling account. Take a look around and see how it can help streamline your business on the go.
