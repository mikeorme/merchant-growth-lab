Ready to get started?
Step 1
Visit the Appstore
Sign up for a Professional selling account, or log in to Seller Central if you already have one. From the main menu in Seller Central, select Apps and Services, then Selling Partner Appstore.
Step 2
Discover solutions
Search by keyword, filter by category, or browse by curated collections. Filter further by supported programs—such as Amazon Business, Fulfilment by Amazon (FBA), and Global Selling—languages , currency, reviews, and more.
Step 3
Evaluate your options
Select an app to review more information on its detail page, including specific features, a “Who should use this?” recommendation, pricing, and more. Look for the “Notifications in Seller Central” badge to spot apps with this capability enabled.
Step 4
Get the app
Select Visit Developer Website to go to the third-party’s site where you can purchase or download the app.
Step 5
Authorize the app
From the app’s detail page, select Authorize Now to authorize the app to access your selling data.

You can also authorize, re-authorize, disable authorization, or opt out of notifications in Manage Your Apps in Seller Central.
