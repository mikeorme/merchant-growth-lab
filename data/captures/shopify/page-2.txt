Shopify is a tech company, not a bank. We partner with Stripe Payments Company for money transmission services and account services with funds held at Fifth Third Bank N.A., Member FDIC. Shopify Balance Visa® Commercial cards are powered by Stripe and issued by Celtic Bank pursuant to a license from Visa U.S.A. Inc. Shopify Credit Visa® Commercial Credit cards are powered by Stripe and issued by Celtic Bank pursuant to a license from Visa U.S.A. Inc. See Issuing Bank Terms and Program Terms.

The Balance earnings rate is a reward provided by Shopify and is not interest. The rate is variable, subject to change without notice, and accurate as of November 19, 2026. The reward accrues daily and is paid monthly in the form of a credit to your Balance account. ACH limits may apply. See Reward Terms. Stores on Shopify Plus are rewarded with 3.54%, and all other plans receive 2.52%.

"Cashback" refers to rewards earned as a percentage discount on eligible purchases. Earn 3% cashback as a statement credit on up to US$250,000 of annual eligible purchases in your monthly top spend category—either marketing, fulfillment, or wholesale, and 1% cashback thereafter. Earn 1% cashback on the other two spend categories. Restrictions apply. See Program Terms for details. By default, you are opted in to paying your full statement balance by an automatic debit from your designated bank account within 1 month to avoid fees. You have the option to switch to making daily payments from your sales and take up to 10 months to pay your full statement balance, for a fee. If payment is not received in full within 10 months, a 2% monthly late fee will apply to your past-due balance.
