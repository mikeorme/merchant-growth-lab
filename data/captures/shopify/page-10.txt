B2B FEATURES

The features you need, built right into the core of Shopify

Every feature below is included on all plans starting from Basic, unless marked otherwise. Compare B2B features by plan.
Personalized buyer experiences
Personalize your storefront, checkout, and accounts experience for each customer.
Company profiles
Represent multiple buyers and locations with unique payment terms and user permissions.
B2B catalogs
Set custom pricing and product availability for groups of buyers through B2B markets. Basic, Grow, and Advanced include up to 3 catalog assignments, while Shopify Plus has no limit.
Customer-specific catalogs
Offer customer-specific pricing by assigning catalogs directly to a company or company location.

Requires Shopify Plus

Quantity rules
Create customer-specific conditional rules for products and variants, including minimums, maximums, and increments.
Volume pricing
Easily implement quantity price breaks to get your customers buying in larger quantities.
Store personalization
Optimize the B2B experience by surfacing relevant content to the right audience in one store.

Requires Grow plan or higher

Headless storefronts
Gain total control over your buyer experience with Shopify APIs and developer tools along with your tech stack.
Custom B2B solutions
Build the solution your business needs with our suite of B2B APIs and compatible apps.
Customizable buyer portal
Use apps to customize the customer accounts experience with new features and functionality.
3P authentication support
Offer customers a consistent login experience across all your platforms by enabling your existing identity provider.

Requires Shopify Plus

Optimized workflows
Modernize your operations with powerful automation tools to streamline processes across all your brands and sites.
Workflow automations
Shopify Flow, our proprietary automation tool, now does more for B2B with support for companies and company locations, so you can automate new account setup, send invoices, and more.
Company account requests
Using Shopify Forms, add a customizable account request form to your store. Submitted information is automatically saved as a company.
Sales rep permissions
Add sales reps as staff in the admin with the permissions they need to only access assigned customers and place orders on their behalf.

Requires Grow plan or higher

Checkout to draft
Simplify workflows and quotes with the ability to accept draft orders on your online store for you to review and approve before fulfilling.
B2B-optimized theme
Accelerate your store setup with Trade, our out-of-the-box theme designed for wholesale, and pre-built with our advanced B2B features.
PO numbers
Capture PO numbers on orders and at checkout, which are then visible in the admin, within customer accounts, and on the Orders API.
Unified drafts experience
Checkout customizations now automatically apply to draft orders, ensuring a consistent experience for sales staff in the admin.
Self-serve purchasing
Scale your operations with online buyer purchasing and order tracking for hands-off account management.
Quick bulk ordering
Show buyers a list of all products and variants so they can easily order sizes, colors, and more from product pages or a separate order form.
Flexible payment options
Streamline the order process with manual payment methods, payment terms, and the ability to send invoices for payment.
Easy reordering
Encourage repeat orders by making it easy for buyers to reorder past purchases right from their customer account.
Vaulted credit cards
Speed up payment by securely storing customer credit cards on file for use at checkout, on draft orders, and when paying invoices.
PayPal payments
B2B customers can now pay for orders and invoices with PayPal, with their payments automatically reconciled in the admin.
Custom discounts
Offer promotional discounts on top of wholesale prices, tailored to your B2B buyers.
VAT invoices
Easily generate, store, and display compliant VAT invoices for B2B customers in the EU and UK with Shopify Tax.
Self-serve returns
Allow B2B customers to initiate returns directly through their customer account, and manage returns directly in the admin.
