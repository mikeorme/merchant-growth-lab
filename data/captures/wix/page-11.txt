What is a professional business email?

A professional business email uses your own custom domain, yourname@yourbusiness.com, instead of a generic provider like Gmail or Yahoo. It signals to customers, partners and clients that you're a real, credible business. Your email domain should always match your website domain: when someone receives a message from you, that consistency builds instant brand recognition and trust. If you don't have a domain yet, Wix domains lets you get a free domain for a year with any annual website plan — so your professional email address and site are set up together from the start.
