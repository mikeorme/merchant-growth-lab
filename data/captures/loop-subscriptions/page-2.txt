How much does Loop cost?

Loop has four plans: Free Forever (up to 50 active subscriptions), Starter ($99/month + 1.0% per transaction, no per-order fee), Pro ($399/month + 0.75% per transaction, no per-order fee), and Enterprise (custom pricing). All paid plans are billed month-to-month with a 14-day free trial.

Does Loop charge a per-order fee?

No. Loop's paid plans charge a percentage of transaction volume (1.0% on Starter, 0.75% on Pro) with no per-order flat fee. At 10,000 subscription orders/month and $50 AOV, Loop Pro works out to approximately $4,149/month.

Does Loop charge setup fees, migration fees, or hidden charges?

No. Loop does not charge setup, migration, or onboarding fees. White-glove migration is included on every paid plan, and payment processing fees (Stripe, PayPal, Braintree, Authorize.net, Shopify Payments) are handled directly by the payment processor. See how migration works on Loop →

Does Loop charge for add-ons or have hidden fees?

No. Loop's published pricing includes all core features at each tier — bundles, cancellation flows, smart dunning, customer portal, analytics, and dedicated CSM on Pro plans — with no separate add-on charges.

What kind of support does Loop offer?

Loop offers chat support for all users. Pro plans include a dedicated Customer Success Manager and a shared Slack channel. As of May 2026, Loop holds a 4.9-star rating across 650+ Shopify App Store reviews.

What will Loop cost at my order volume?

Loop's paid plans are $99/month + 1.0% (Starter) and $399/month + 0.75% (Pro), with no per-order flat fee on either tier. At 10,000 subscription orders/month and $50 AOV, Loop Pro works out to approximately $4,149/month — month-to-month billing, no term commitment. See full comparison →
